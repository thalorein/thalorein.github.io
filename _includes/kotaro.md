A former pirate turned adventurer-spellcaster, Kotaro is guided by the promise he made to his childhood friend, Ryuutaro, who went missing during the 7th Umbral Calamity. To this end, he set a goal for himself: to explore the world and its hidden wonders.

Having grown up around the port city of Limsa Lominsa, he loves the high seas and the smell of the salty wind. 
He also enjoys strong sake, long naps, and stirring up trouble.
