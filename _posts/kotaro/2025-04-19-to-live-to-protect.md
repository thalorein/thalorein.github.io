> Aftermath of "A Knight's Calling", written with the foresight that the WOL makes a promise with Venat in another time.
{: .note }

"Fuck!"

Kotaro unceremoniously slams his practice sword and shield to the ground. A loud _clang_! rattles and echos all around the enclosed arena. He pants, frustrated at his lack of progress.

"Fuck it, fuck it all to the seven hells!"

He slams his right fist into the nearest wall he finds, it's force reverberating back out. Sweat drops down his forehead and onto the ground.

He's not sure if he can do it, doesn't understand how anyone does it, doesn't understand how Haurchefant does it - using a sworld and shield that is. More specifically, a shield.

Barely a week after the events at the Vault, Kotaro had enlisted with the Gladiator's Guild, driven by the memory of his brother and wanting to protecting things like he once did.

As someone who tended to prefer quick dodges and parries, and swiftly throwing all weight off him as quickly as he can, guarding and taking a strong bash into his shield was something he had not quite gotten used to, despite training for weeks.

His sword play was fine. Exceptional even, which was surprising given that the only thing he'd swung around in months besides a boot knife and a lance was arcane magicks. And using a lance was nothing like swinging a short sword - it's reach and weight were different, requiring entirely different techniques and finesse.

But deep down, he knows what's holding him back, as much as he does not want to acknowledge it.

It's a problem he had carried over from his pirating days. It had all started with mundanity. The usual practice sessions they had on the deck - held mostly for them to practice whatever they'd like. He had been a newbie at brawling then, practicing with clumsy hits, and then it happened. His sparring partner threw a punch that was going to connect, and by sheer instinct, he had moved to block it with his left arm - unguarded, forgetting the basics of utilizing his chakra - which was a mistake. He'd needed a healer and a cast then, but he never attempted hand to hand combat since.

It's silly, and he knows it. It's such an old injury that has long healed, and yet it has only affected the way he battles since then. He knows at some point that he should attempt the ways of the Monk again, simply because he needs the flexibility and fluidity to adapt to any scenario he's thrown into. But now was not such a time. He was not ready. In fact, he doesn't know when he will be ready.

He stares at the shield which he has thrown carelessly on the ground. It stares back at him. He shudders, and swings his head away from the sight.

His left arm trembles. He grabs it with his other arm, and holds it tight. He remembers the haunting day that bolt of light pierced through his sworn brother's shield.

_To live, to protect, to serve._

It was Haurchefant's dream. And now, it's his as well. Haurchefant had kept his vows as a knight to the very end. And now Kotaro walks in his footsteps to keep his memory alive. He cannot forget, _he won't_.

He squeezes his eyes shut, hanging his head. He feels the crushing weight of failure - the inability to save his sworn brother, the inability to even use the shield. He'd made an oath - they would protect each other's backs. But he'd failed, his brother was gone.

He remembers the look on Count Edmont's face. The grief, the sorrow. The love he had for his son, illegitimate though he may be. He'd left shortly after - Edmont had been so devasted, and Kotaro could not bear the thought of facing him for even a moment longer. For all the help and succor Haurchefant had brought them by helping them find their airship, and giving them temporary reprieve as political refugees, how had he repaid Edmont's goodwill? If the Count never forgave him, he cannot begrudge him for doing so, for he repaid his kindness with death.

How can he do it? How can he protect anyone else like this, when he can't even save a single person? How can he prevent anyone else from dying? He's not sure if he can do it. He's the warrior of light, and if there's one thing that he's sure of now, it's that death and chaos follows wherever he goes.

Just barely a summer ago, he'd been just another adventurer, content with his lot in life. And yet. He was chosen by Hydaelyn. Everything changed drastically in a blink of an eye. Before he knew it, he was fighting Primals. Getting involved in awful politics, things that he'd never thought he'd ever been involved in. All he could think of was, why? Why was he chosen? Despite everyone calling the Echo the blessing of the mothercrystal, was it in fact, a curse? There were so many inhabitants in this vast land called Eorzea - why him specifically?

He'd hoped that by picking up the sword and shield, he'd learn the answers he needed. But nothing had changed. All he'd done was pick up a new skill, but he was still just Kotaro. Answers still eluded him and the universe, echoing it's silence as a conspirator.

But whatever he feels, he knows. He's the warrior of light. He has a duty to fulfill. The mantle hangs heavy, but while his heart still beats, the job must still be done. Even if all else seems impossible, even if only death follows in his wake, he has to make the impossible happen.

Picking up the sword wasn't the only thing he had in mind. He had other plans to further his lance skills, but more specifically, his arcanima. The skills that have been with him the longest as an adventurer. And fortunately for him, he's practiced enough that he knows how to devise more arcane geometries and formulas, even those related to the forbidden. He just has to attempt them in the dead of the night, probably at Silvertear Falls, where aether is abundant.

Anything that will boost his array of battle skills quickly, he'd do it, no matter how far down the rabbit hole he falls.

"Kotaro, let's try that again," his sparring buddy calls out, seemingly oblivious to the turmoil in his head.

He bends down and picks up his sword and shield slowly. His arms are still trembling a little. But he has to attempt, does he not? He's alive, and that means endless opportunities for improvement. He cannot repay Haurchefant's sacrifice with complacency now.

He crouches low, and gets back into his battle ready stance. Taking a deep breath, he leaps and attempts yet another shield bash. He feels his left arm shiver and his instincts yelling at him to redirect the force up, but he grits his teeth and holds his current position. Against the onslaught of his opponent, he attempts to resist all the temptation to do anything else, to hold on for as long as he can. He grits his teeth, desperate to overcome his weaknesses.

_For Haurchefant, he has to try._
