Everything was a blur.

The Scions, his closest comrades, were gone. Scattered like leaves in the wind, or worse. He didn't know who had survived. If anyone had.

Only Alphinaud and Tataru remained. The silence left in the wake of the bloody banquet was deafening. Even if he had not been especially close to them, he'd felt solidarity with them, a similar way it'd been with his old pirate crew.

It felt like the world had been ripped out from under him. Like he'd lost everything in a single night.

Now he sat in the Intercessory, hunched over a mug of hot chocolate. It was sweet and warm - completely at odds with the maelstrom raging in his mind.

Haurchefant sat nearby, calm as ever, the hearthlight catching in his silvery blue hair.

After a long moment, he spoke, voice light and kind.


"Why did you become an adventurer?"

Kotaro didn’t answer right away. But he welcomed the small talk all the same - it was a good distraction. A respite from the turmoil of the day's events. He stared into the swirling steam rising from his mug, watching it curl and vanish.

Then, he responded softly.


"Ain't in it fer t' fortune or glory."


A pause. His voice dropped lower.


*"I'm chasin' t' shadow o' the past."*
