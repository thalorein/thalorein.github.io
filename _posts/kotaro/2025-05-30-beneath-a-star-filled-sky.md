"Who goes there?!"

Kotaro heard a sharp voice as he strode into view. The owner of said voice visibly relaxing her shoulders as he walked up and sat beside her on the edge of the cliffs of Outer La Noscea, outside of a Maelstrom outpost. 

"Gods, today is such a mess isn't it?" Alisaie whispered softly, looking up into the sky. 

It was quiet, with only the sound of the leaves rustling in the wind, and they were surrounded by nothing but darkness and the twinkling of stars. On any other day, it would have seen it as Nymeia's blessing, but tonight, all it offered was cold comfort and loneliness.

"I had hoped that we would be able to save Gabu's parents..." she continued, looking to her side, where Gabu stood listlessly, staring into nothingness. "He's just a child... And his parents... gone... Fate... it's too cruel isn't it? My heart aches for him."

A pause. Looking back at the sky, she continued her story.

"I came to Eorzea, thinking that by following in Grandfather's footsteps, I might be able to understand why he did what he did. There were so many who made me angry, and question why Grandfather did what he did. But there were many others, whom I felt a kinship with... whom I was unable to save."

Then, looking down, she raised her palms up, and stared at them, before clenching them into fists.

"I asked myself, what was it all for? Would I always be doomed to fail? Should I even have tried, knowing what awaited them?"

Finally, she released her fingers, letting all the tension out.

"There were days I faltered, and was afraid. But then I thought of my brother, Urianger, and you...and then I remembered Grandfather's words."

"To ignore the plight of those one who might conceivably save is not wisdom - it is indolence. We must all protect that which we hold most dear in the manner of our own choosing."

"...We have to try, do we not?" her voice sounded heavy and unsure, her eyes wet with unshed tears. But still, she attempted a tiny smile, and faced Kotaro at last. Kotaro could only marvel at her strength, despite her sadness over the events that had transpired earlier that day.

It was a rare moment of vulnerability, but despite that, Kotaro felt nothing but a sense of closeness with Alisaie. Not unlike what he felt with Haurchefant and Estinien. It was a rare thing to feel, something he had not felt with many others.

He thought back to all the Scions he'd met in the past few months. Minfilia, gone. He didn't talk with nor see much of Yda and Papalymo. Alphinaud, who made him feel a little off kilter with the way he used placed him on a pedestral and made decisions for him, though definitely getting better in recent days. Tataru, who reminded him of his mother in some way - doing what they did well, but never bridging the distance. Urianger - someone whom he never really understood due to his propensity to speak in riddles. Thancred, who kept his problems close to his chest. He might have easygoing and flirty nature once, but such was a deflection for what was truly hidden below. And now? He was haunted by his demons and walled off, mostly brooding by himself.

*I have fought tooth and nail for the people I hold dear - done everything in my power to save them...and I have failed. Learn to live with it. I have.* He remembered the harsh words spoken to Emmanellain back that day. Trying so hard to convince himself not to relieve his mistakes, but it was clear to Kotaro that he was failing at it. And as much as Kotaro wished he could help, he knew instinctively that it was something that Thancred had to work through himself. 

Alisaie however - her straightforward and explosive nature had endeared itself to him since their first encounter. Idealistic like her brother, but in a complete opposite manner. Her brother, gifted with his words and always trying to woo over others with grand words, the mark of someone with a flair for politics. But, with her, it was more actions than words. Her quickness in launching herself straight into danger to save soneone. Her grief, raw and open, when brave Gabu fell to tempering. Her earnest want to save Gabu, which resonated with him deeply.

It was then he realised - she didn't elevate him, but treated him as an equal. A living being with flaws, with strengths, and even weaknesses. And she wasn't afraid to share in them, be it in good or bad times. Someone she could be honest with her troubles, but also there to slap them back to their feet. Just like Estinien and Haurchefant, who saw him for who he was.

Kotaro turned to look at the baby Kobold, who had followed Alisaie languidly, nary a word out of his mouth since the encounter with Titan. After a brief moment, he responded, giving her a warm smile.

"If it's ye, Alisaie, I’ve no doubt ye’ll find a way t'save Gabu in the end. 'm certain o' it."
