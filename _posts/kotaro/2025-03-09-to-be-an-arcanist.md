“Kotaro.”

Kotaro looks up from his meddling to turn to Mikh’to, standing right behind him, "That's me, what's up, Mikh'to?"

“What do you do as an arcanist?” Mikh’to asks, a thoughtful expression on his face as he stares at his carbuncle.

“Yer interested?” Kotaro grins, grabbing Mikh’to by the wrist, and proceeds to sprint towards the nearest aethernet shard. “Come! Me uncle, K’rhid, is the Arcanist Guildmaster ‘ere in Limsa! He can teach ye ‘verything!”

“Your… uncle?” Mikh’to looks confused as he is dragged at top speed to their destination.

“Hi Thubyrgeim! Is uncle ‘round?” Kotaro greets her excitedly, without looking at his surroundings.

Thubyrgeim sighs, and shakes her head in resignation. Guess not. Instead, a nearby female miqo’te spins around at his voice, and yells in surprise.

“Tiqa! It’s you!” The pink-haired miqo’te exclaims, running up to their side. “Have you learnt any new and interesting spells? I wanna expand my arsenal!”

“No no, not yet, Lyhia. I’m ‘ere to introduce me friend, Mikh’to, to the guild! He says he wanna learn the way o’ the arcanist!”

“I didn’t-”

“A new student!? Thubyrgeim! We need a new grimoire! Now!!!”

In a flurry of events, Mikh’to finds himself dragged at their pace, a grimoire in his hands and a carbuncle by his side.


--


“So, how ‘as it?” Kotaro grins, as he walks out of the guild with Mikh’to after a long day at the guild, the sun setting serenely in the distance.

“It was… okay…” Mikh’to has that far away look on his face. “So… K’lyhia is from your tribe?”

Kotaro freezes, realization hits him, a slightly embarrassed look on his face “Ah, right. Yeah, she’s me cousin. I guess proper introductions are next.” He takes a deep breath and psyches himself up. “K’tiqa Tia. Nice to meet ye, Mikh’to.”

“And Kotaro..?”
“It’s just ‘nother name ‘cause ye know miqo’te names are hard fer most people.” Yet the far away look in his eyes says otherwise.

Mikh’to doesn’t push the matter, a soft smile on his face, “Then, I look forward to working with you, K’tiqa.”
