"Kotaro! Wake up!"

Kotaro jolts awake at the sound of the familiar voice. He'd been taking a kip under the shade of an apple tree while waiting for his closest friend to appear.

"Let's play!" his friend yells excitedly, tugging his arm in an attempt to dislodge him from his comfortable spot on the ground.

This had been their song and dance for the past several moons, ever since this kid had appeared one day out of the blue, at his usual napping spot. He wore clothes that were unusual by Vylbrand's standards - long robed and filled with earthly, warm colours. This kid - Ryuutaro he was called - wasn't native to these lands, displaced here by the war with the Garleans in his homeland. He's more amazed that they even managed to land in Vylbrand, considering they came from the Far East - the Drown in the Sea of Ash being known for its rather treacherous waters.

Kotaro lets him pull him to his feet, brushing dirt from his shorts as stretches his legs and unfurls his tail, not quite fully grown yet, his furry ears flicking in the process.

They start their daily routine then. Running around the hills, hiding in wine barrels, climbing trees, observing coeurls, megalocrabs and jackals from the distance. They end the high of the afternoon by lazily riding the swings hanging from a tree near Oschon's Torch, a lighthouse near the small seaside town of Moraby Drydocks.

"Say, is _hotaru_ a thing 'round here?" Ryuutaro suddenly asks, a curious lilt to his voice.

"Ho…taru…?" Kotaro asks, puzzled. He wonders if it's some sort of Far Eastern ornament.

"Y'know, tiny flies that only appear at night. With lights in their arse," Ryuutaro says by way of explanation.

"Ah, _fireflies_." Kotaro's seen them in books, even if he's never seen one in actuality. He digs his memory for any scrap of information he can recall.

"Ne'er had a reason t' stay out late, but the rumors go that if ye camp out th' outskirts o' th' Drydocks, ye'll see summat," Kotaro finally says.

There's no response for a while, Ryuutaro kicking his legs in midair, lost in the mire of his thoughts.

"Back in Yanxia… you'd see tons of them in summer," Ryuutaro says, a wistful tone seeping into his voice. "I miss it. Home, I mean. I hope the war will end soon."

Kotaro cannot imagine what it's like to be so far away from home, so he keeps his mouth shut. He's silent for a good long moment. But eventually, his inner curiosity wins over.

"I'd like to hop on a skiff meself and jaunt 'round the lands past Vylbrand… One day," he muses absentmindedly. Not that he's sure if the skiff would survive a maelstrom out in the Drown.

This startles a guffawing laughter out of his friend.

"Then, let's visit my homeland someday. I promise it's beautiful." Ryuutaro smiles, his bright blue eyes shimmering in the accent of the sunset, fluffy brown hair swaying gently in the breeze of the sea. He lifts his arm, and holds out his pinky finger.

Today, the air has a salty smell, like it always is this near to the ocean.

Kotaro takes a deep breath, and gives an impish grin. Then, he swipes a quick nip of his pinky and returns the gesture.
