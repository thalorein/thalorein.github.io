“Mikh’to, could ye introduce me to the Conjurers’ Guild? I’d like to try me hands at conjury.”

Mikh’to turns and casts Kotaro a dubious look, hands stopping mid-task to contemplate the possibility. Having seen how Kotaro heals in alliance raids as a scholar, he is extremely doubtful that Kotaro will have any talent at conjury. After a long moment, with Kotaro’s sparkling enthusiasm plain on his face, he finally gives in with a sigh. 

“Sure, let’s go,” he says, putting his things aside and heading for the nearest Aetheryte. A day of learning basics shouldn't be too bad, right? Surely he can manage even a Cure I?

He introduces Kotaro to E-Sumi-Yan, standing aside to watch the guildmaster explain the history and philosophy behind conjury. In the end he decides that standing around like a worried friend isn’t going to help anything and goes around Gridania visiting old friends.

--

“... and now, try feeling the elements in the air around you. Channel that feeling into a Stone.” 
Mikh’to returns hours later to find an exasperated E-Sumi-Yan trying to teach the most basic spell to a rather befuddled Kotaro who is trying his damndest to hypothetically feel the air around him. 

A rather bemused Sylphie, who has been watching them the entire time, walks up to Mikh’to and whispers into his ear, “E-Sumi said that your friend’s like I was before so he tried to call for my help. Except the difference is I knew what I was doing but he’s completely clueless.”

Kotaro ends up casting using his own aether reserves yet again.

“Let us stop here,” sighs E-Sumi-Yan, slouching his shoulders and giving up completely, “it is futile to continue attempting this without repercussions.”

“Figures…” Kotaro says sheepishly while scratching his ears.

--

“Man, ‘m beat. Ye know any good places fer eatin’?” Kotaro asks Mikh’to as they leave the Conjurers’ Guild. Mikh’to thinks over the question, “We can go to the Carline Canopy. Mother Miounne would know best.”

Kotaro excitedly recounts his experience with E-Sumi Yan as they walk to their destination, still rather bemused at the elusive elements that conjurers are attuned to. The Carline Canopy is fairly empty, given the odd timing for a meal, the few patrons there enjoying their afternoon tea and snacks. 

“Mikh’to!”

He turns towards the caller at the sound of the other’s name. A female Keeper in server garb with the brightest smile on her face comes running up to them, throwing her arms around the other man. 

“Rhaya.” Mikh’to’s voice is noticeably softer, a hiss and a throaty sound follows the name. Huntspeak; Kotaro will never fully understand it but he is certain that it is a simple greeting call this time. Mikh’to returns her hug, rubbing his nose against her ear with soft purrs.

“It’s been so long, how have you been?”

Mikh’to trills in reply, not moving from his apparent comfortable position of nosing her ear, earning him her laughter. 

“I see, I see, it’s nice to see you again too.”

“Uh… ye gonna introduce yer lover or summat?” Kotaro asks puzzledly, scratching his head. He has never seen Mikh’to this open or relaxed with anyone, not even with that rag-tag group that call themselves his brothers. 

The fair lass pulls away from the hug, amused, “As honored as I am to be thought of as such, I’m just his sister.”

“Huh… didn’t know ye had a sister,” Kotaro marvels at the revelation, “I thought it was just Zan and the others.” 

“Sisters,” Mikh’to corrects him, “and you didn’t ask. It’s different; she’s my real sister.”

“Come now, I’ll get you boys something to eat,” Rhaya shows them to an empty table.

“How do ye know we’re ‘ere for that?” Kotaro almost thinks she’s a mind-reader. Mikh’to sighs, “Your scent says it all.”
