*Aymeric was bad, but at least he was cordial,* Kotaro thought bitterly, recalling the very first hostile parley he'd had with the Lord Commander of Ishgard. 

The neutral and unassuming stare, the smile he gave that did not reach anyone's eyes. It gave away zero weakness. It was the perfect politician's facade. It was unnerving, to say the least. Politics was never something he liked getting wrapped in, and the atmosphere of the meeting was suffocating. He and Alphinaud had left the meeting feeling left out of their breadth, and throughly played by the him. 

He hadn't had many good impressions of Ishgardians thus far, save for Haurchefant, the blessed kind soul, who went out of his way to help them with their fallen airship, when honestly, he didn't need to.

But meeting Estinien... was a whole other level. 

"That Ishgard would resort to sending coin-starved adventurers after me... I know not whether to laugh or feel insulted."

He spun his head and chanced a glance at the fellow. Clan in full armor from head to toe, but with a quick tilt of his head, he saw the dragoon's eyes for a split second, before it disappeared again under the angle of the helmet. It was a stormy grey. But yet, it was cold, dark and empty. Like staring into an abyss, where nothing of humanity seemed to have remained.

Coupled with the amount of derisiveness and disgust in his tone had the fur on his tail standing on ends. And fuck. Who said he was coin-starved?! 

"Ye'd better check yer gil a'fore ye go callin' others coin-starved!" growled Kotaro, unable to resist his desire to be snarky.

This was probably the worst Ishgardian he had met by far - the worst most others had done was shoo him away with their seemingly polite high-born words. 

But this fellow was not only downright rude and condescending. He was also flighty - he instantly jumped away after delivering his insults! The least he could have done was at least finish a whole damn argument, even if they had to resort to trading blows! He really hated it when people left loose ends untied. Settle things here and now, and let things be even between them, never to be raised again - such was his way of doing things. Kotaro wasn't sure if he had ever disliked anyone more on their very first meeting, and yet, he couldn't pinpoint specifically what about this man really ticked him off. At least, not yet.

...

How in the seven hells had he even fallen into this mess in the first place? 

...Right, Ywain had requested a favor from him to help his friend. Why the fuck did he agree to it? He sighed a world weary sigh, as he trodded back to Alberic to deliver onto him the events of the day.

"I was once his teacher," Alberic had admitted softly after Kotaro had finished his report on the day's events.

But the sadness in his eyes told his tale - there was a far deeper history between them than just student and master.  That much, Kotaro could glean. He suspected that Alberic cared for this idiot very greatly, but he didn't know why.

He would later learn that Estinien had been the sole survivor of Ferndale, a village razed to the ground by Nidhogg. And the person who found and raised him as his own was none other than Alberic himself.

-- 

The second meeting between them at Boulder Downs went no better.

Estinien spoke coldly and logically for most of the conversation. About why he stole the eye, how he wanted to divert Nidhogg away from the city. Most of it tactical and sound - spoken just as a top ranking dragoon with training in military tactics should, and probably the best way to minimize casualties. But something about the way he spoke just didn't sit right with Kotaro, until Alberic cut in.

"Y-you mean to make a bait of yourself?!" Alberic asked, shock colouring his tone. Then a realization hit him, and his next words were careful, and subdued. "... Nay, there is more to this than you let on. You seek vengeance for Ferndale."

"Nidhogg took everything from me - now I shall take everything from him, even if it means my life." Estinien responded, a calm but determined tone in his voice.

*This guy lives for the sole purpose of killing Nidhogg,* Kotaro thought uneasily. He didn't like the idea of someone being so self-sacrificial - living only for revenge.


"The eye works in ways men can ill comprehend, but this much is plain: in choosing two azure dragoons in one generation, it means for us to join together in common cause. Adventurer. You have heard my resolution. Now I ask that you lend me your power - aye, the power of the dragon. Our only chance to send the great wyrm to eternal slumber."

His impassioned speech was a breaking point for Kotaro. Not once in the entire process had Estinien even bothered to learn about who he was, other than the fact that the Dragon's Eye had picked him to be the unlikely second Azure Dragoon.

Which selfish idiot makes someone work for their goals while barely knowing anything about them ?! He could bet a thousand gil that the guy didn't even know his name. And secondly, he didn't even give him a chance to say anything about the entire matter!

Not to mention, Ishgard had isolated herself through the centuries, never helping with matters in the rest of Eorzea. What gave him the right to make demands and order for help with their own internal problems? 

And, though he had left his past behind, his mind still helpfully supplied,  *pirates bow to nobody* - they were free, they lived as they liked. It had been his world, the way he lived, and not so much different from his lifestyle as an adventurer now. He would not be ordered to do anything on anyone's whim, save for the fact that Hydaelyn already had their grasp on him.

But he grit his teeth and kept that thought to himself. It was either helping Estinien or letting that idiot die and possibly letting an entire city be razed to the ground. 

It was also clear that all Estinien cared about were his own goals, his own duty. If he had to drag someone else to the bottom of Llymlaen's deep sea to get it done, he would do it. He did not give two shits about anyone else's opinion or what they thought of him. He was someone who worked alone.


"Yer a right pain in the fuckin' arse, an' Llymlaen knows I'd sooner say no just t' spite ye... but, I'll do it. Ain't no way I'm lettin' ye toss yer life overboard like that. *Never while I'm breathin'.*" Kotaro emphasized the last sentence, though he was really loathe to be working with Estinien at all.

It sounded idealistic even to Kotaro himself, especially since he wasn't the sort of person to think beyond his immediate circle of companions. Camaradie mattered to him - it's what kept him going when times were tough as a pirate, and it's what kept him going even when times were tough with the Scions. He would not stoop so low to abandon someone who needed his help and he could provide it.

"I'm simply choosing the lesser of two evils," was all Estinien responded with, before leaving.

As he left Boulder Downs, he started to contemplate. Looking back at his training thus far with the other dragoons, he felt so conflicted. How could one man be so strong, such an asshole, and still be admired by the other dragoons? Heusteinne for sure, seemed to really look up to him. Alberic had such a huge soft spot for him as well.

Still though, Kotaro was not one to shirk from a task given to him. Taking Estinien's zealous ramblings in his stride, he trained ever harder to quickly complete the trials required to obtain the blessed Dragoon mail.

--

Then, the pinnacle of the drama came.

"Kotaro, I need to tell you the truth of Ferndale," Alberic said to him one fine day, and he began to recall his sorry tale.

Kotaro should have been more shocked by the truth, but he wasn't. He felt like a mixed bag of morbol's spit. He doesn't begrudge the man for his terror in the moment of Nidhogg's rage. But he cannot imagine what Estinien would feel upon learning the truth. He man had been so intense in their every meeting, he's sure that this would all end with nothing but tragedy.

They had to find him, and quickly, before things spiralled out of hand. But when they met him, his spear pointed straight at them, they realised it. *They were too late.* 

"The Eye revealed to me the truth - a truth so damning as to undo a lifetime of trust. I loved you as a father ... but I can ill forgive you for Ferndale." Estinien was getting more and more heated by the minute, and Kotaro could feel the slow buildup of an oppressive and ominous aether around him. This... was not good. 

"Calm yourself, Estinien! Your rage renders you vulnerable to the dragon's influence!" Alberic had pleaded, hoping his voice would reach Estinien. But he paid them no heed, blind to his rage now. 

There was only one thing to do - fight it all out. Kotaro stepped in front of Alberic, his bichromatic eyes steely with determined. However, this seemed to make Estinien's temper flare even further.

"In you, I thougt I had found kindred, someone beside whom I could fight the great wyrm. It is with a heavy heart that I relinquish that dream. Your affection for this coward has rendered you deaf to your own destiny." Estinien seethed.

"Don't give a damn what 'e done- 'e bloody raised ye, didn't 'e? Ye don't just get t' turn 'round an' piss on that like it means nothin'!" Kotaro responded, pissed to the seven hells.

"Don't you dare speak like you know what I endured!" Estinien jumped, and - clang! - there was the clash of steel, their pair of lances - Gae Bolgs both - locked in place together, both trying to push for the advantage. Kotaro knew that Estinien had the advantage of experience - years of combat experience fighting dragons, cunning and ready for war. Were he calm, he would have been a force to be reckoned with. But now, tempered by his anger, his movements were haphazard, sloppy and heavy handed.

They danced around for a bit, until Kotaro found his opening - stealthily reaching into his boot, and swung out his bootknife, catching Estinien off guard as it flew past his helmet, barely missing his cheek. *A trick that could only catch someone unaware once, and never again.* Then with a rush, he thrust a disembowel straight into Estinien's sternum, knocking him off his breath and feet. Sprawled on the floor, it seemed like this was the end of it. The ominous aether surrounding him seemed to be slowly dispersing.

Kotaro stopped for a moment to catch his breath, before he looked up, realizing what was happening too late, horror flooding him.

The aether now was swirling, more concentrated than before. Estinien's arms raised to the sky, as if in a thrall. "Grant onto me claws and fangs to rend thine enemies!" he intoned in fervor, the tone of his voice taking a different slant.

He couldn't afford to hesitate now. Kotaro focused his aether as well, into the core of himself. And suddenly, he had the visage of an Azure Dragoon, clad in pure white aether. It was calling to him, and he knew then and there what to do. With a shout, he landed his first dragonfire dive, straight into the maw of the sprawling dark aether emitted from Estinien, who could only look in shock. 

What came next was a blinding explosion of light. And just as suddenly as it appeared, it vanished. With it, Estinien was gone to the wind as well.

All Kotaro could think about in that moment was hoping that he'd never have to face Estinien again - he was a right menace and nuisance. No matter that Alberic, shaken though he may be, forgave him for everything that had transpired so far. One simply did not attack their foster parent with such a murderous intent to kill. This guy had serious unresolved issues, and probably not a good use of energy to spend time around. 

The entire matter wrapped up, he left the snowy grounds of Coerthas, hoping that he wouldn't have to come back here so soon.
He'd had enough of this cold, dreary place for now.



Unfortunately, he was forced to take political refuge in Camp Dragonhead just but a week later. No rest for the righteous, was it? He wasn't sure about facing any new world shattering things right after seeing the Sultana of U'ldah assassinated right in front of his very eyes, and also losing most of his comrades, the Scions, in the process. 

But being in Ishgard again meant he'd have to be involved with their troubles, and probably, *Estinien again*. He was not looking forward to that.

*Llymlaen give me strength,* he thought wearily about dealing with such turbulent times.
