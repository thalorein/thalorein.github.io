*Aymeric was bad, but at least he was cordial, even if he was hiding behind a politician's facade.* Kotaro thought bitterly, recalling the very first hostile parley he'd had with the Lord Commander of Isgard. He and Alphinaud had left the meeting feeling left out of their breadth, and throughly played by the politician. He hadn't had many good impressions of Ishgardians thus far, save for Haurchefant, who went out of his way to help them with their fallen airship, when he honestly didn't need to.

But Estinien... he was a whole other level. 

"That Ishgard would resort to sending coin-starved adventurers after me... I know not whether to laugh or feel insulted."

The amount of derisiveness and disgust in his tone had the fur on his tail standing on ends. And fuck. Who said he was coin-starved?! Kotaro wasn't sure if he had ever disliked anyone more on their very first meeting. Not only was this fellow rude, he was also condescending. To make matters worse, he instantly jumped away after delivering his insults! The least he could have done was at least finish a whole damn argument, even if they had to resort to trading blows!

How in the seven hells had he even fallen into this mess in the first place? Right, Ywain had requested a favor from him to help his friend. He sighed a world weary sigh.

-- 

The second meeting went no better.

The moment they had met at Boulder Downs, Estinien had instantly launched into passioned speech and forced Kotaro to help him with defeating Ishgard's long standing nemesis, Nidhogg. Not once in the entire process had Estinien even bothered to learn about who he was, other than the fact that the Dragon's Eye had picked him to be the unlikely second Azure Dragoon.

Which selfish idiot makes someone work for their goals while barely knowing anything about them?! And secondly, he didn't even give him a chance to say anything about the entire matter!

Not to mention, Ishgard had isolated herself through the centuries, never helping with matters in the rest of Eorzea. What gave them the right to make demands and order for help with their own internal problems? 

It was clear that all Estinien cared about were his own goals, his own duty. If he had to drag someone else into it to get it done, he would do it. He did not give two shits about anyone else's opinion or what they thought of him. 

As he left Boulder Downs, he started to contemplate. Looking back at his training thus far with the other dragoons, he felt so conflicted. How could one man be so strong, such an asshole, and still be admired by the other dragoons? Heusteinne for sure, seemed to really look up to him. Alberic - Ywain's friend - the one who had requested his help in the first place, seemed to have a soft spot for him as well.

Still though, Kotaro was not one to shirk from a task given to him. He took Estinien's ramblings in his stride, and trained ever harder to quickly complete the trials required to obtain the blassed Dragoon mail.

--

The pinnacle of the whole drama came when Estinien, controlled by Niddhog due to his rage towards Alberic's lie on what had transpired during his childhood, attacked him. While he managed to defeat him, Kotaro wasn't sure whether it was possible that he could ever like this nuisance of an elezen. No matter that Alberic, shaken though he may be, forgave him for everything that had transpired. One simply did not attack their foster parent with the sudden intent to kill. This guy had serious unresolved issues and a too hot temper. 

The entire matter wrapped up, he left the snowy grounds of Coerthas, hoping that he wouldn't have to come back here so soon.
He'd had enough of this cold, dreary place for now.

However, he was forced to take political refuge in Camp Dragonhead just but a week later. No rest for the righteous, was it? He wasn't sure about facing any new world shattering things right after seeing the Sultana of U'ldah assassinated right in front of his very eyes, and also losing most of his comrades, the Scions, in the process. But being in Ishagard again meant he'd have to be involved with their troubles, and probably, Estinien again.

*May Llymlaen give him the strength to tide him through such turbulent times.*
