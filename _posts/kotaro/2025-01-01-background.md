## Notes
Kota is my very first WoL, created when I started playing FF14 in October 2020. His first canon job is Arcanist and starting at Limsa Lominsia was honestly mind-befuddling to me - I liked the celtic vibe of the city but it was the hardest of the 3 starting cities for me to navigate, due to the many different ways you can get between the different levels.

Kota is meant to be someone who has a very tempered sense of right and wrong, being someone who grew up close to Limsa, a maritime thalassocracy ruled by pirates. Not to mention, he was a pirate before A Realm Reborn. While he has no love for politics and its games, being a one brain-cell cat, he has a great respect the tough decisions that Admiral Merlwyb took in guiding the city state to where it is today. 

He's outoing, a complete contrast to F'ynn, and pretty much begins the story without much baggage. Also, he starts to lose his Lominsan accent as he continues his travels, at least partway through Heavensward. I'm also attempting to do some unreliable narration when it comes to Kota's story because he's not that great at paying attention to everything happening around him.
