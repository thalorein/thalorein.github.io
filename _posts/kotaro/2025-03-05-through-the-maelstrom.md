![Image of Kotaro facing Leviathan for the first time](/assets/img/through-the-maelstrom.jpg)

*"May the Navigator guide and keep you, 'til the seas swallow all."*

The words echoed in his ears as the ship pulled away from the shore, as he steeled himself for the battle ahead.

He may have been a pirate once, but even the most seasoned sailors harbored a deep fear of the sea’s wrath.  

The sea - vast enough to offer boundless freedom.  
The sea - deeper still, with dangers enough to humble any man.  
On a choppy day, tidal waves huge enough to swallow them all.  

The thought of facing the monstrous sea beast that was Leviathan was nothing short of terrifying. It wasn’t just the sheer size or fury of the creature that chilled him — it was also the fact that it was a Primal, an ancient force of the Sahagins' zealous devotion given form. This was his first and only chance to bring it down, and the weight of that truth pressed on him like the depths of the seas themselves. Failing which, the entire port of Limsa Lominsa would be swallowed by tidal waves created by none other than this giant serpent itself. Not that he was alone though - he was with a party of seven other adventurers, all with some sort of protection against tempering.

The battle, unfortunately, started off on the wrong foot.

A sudden slap of Leviathan’s tail sent the entire platform lurching, knocking him off balance and slamming him hard into the railings. As if that wasn’t enough, he’d strayed too close to the tank and got slapped by a massive water bubble straight to the face - knocking the wind out of him and dragging a good chunk of his focus with it. It left him somewhat disoriented for a good minute or so, before he could finally catch his bearings. Casting a Ruin spell had never been harder. The platform pitched and bucked with every swell of the waves, threatening to throw off his aim with every pulse of the sea.

Then, Leviathan summoned Gyre Spumes, and some Sahagins leapt onto the platform. Dealing with the Sahagins were a simple enough affair, but the Gyre Spumes? They were a fucking pain - they burst and popped with a spray of saltwater that agitated his tail and left an annoying itchiness on his skin.

But before he could completely get over the Gyre Spumes, Leviathan suddenly shifted tactics. With a low, guttural roar, it dove beneath the waves — then came surging across the platform in violent bursts, its massive body slicing through the arena like a water jet. The attacks came fast and unpredictable, keeping everyone scrambling to stay on their feet, the unlucky ones slipping around as water wooshed past them.

For a moment, he stood frozen, trying to make sense of the sudden change. Then, over the crashing water and shouts of confusion, a voice cut through the chaos.

"The shield! Activate the shield! Now!"

It came from one of the veteran adventurers, his tone sharp with urgency - someone whose battle instincts had been forged and honed through countless battles. That snapped him into motion. He scrambled to yank the lever that activated the elemental converter. And just in time too - as a shimmering barrier flickered to life, a roaring whirlpool surged around them, threating to break the platform apart. Never had he been more relieved for the existence of arcane magicks. Yet even amidst the maelstrom, he found himself captivated by the mesmerizing beauty of the water swirling in strong, radiant arcs around him.

Thankfully, Leviathan didn’t unleash any more surprise tactics after that. But wearing it down was a slow, grueling ordeal, and exhaustion was setting in fast. Everyone was running on fumes. Morale was dropping. Preventable mistakes started occurring. Then, just as it seemed they were all about to collapse, Leviathan let out a piercing screech — and in an instant, its massive form dissolved into a spray of droplets, released into thin air.

It was over. It was over. That's all that mattered.

He was exhausted, and all he wanted to do was catch up on a good long nap. No more seafaring adventures for the next week or so.
