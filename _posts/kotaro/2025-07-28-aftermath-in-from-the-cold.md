> Setting: Aftermath of "In From the Cold"
{: .note }

The sound that assaulted his nose was familiar yet unfamiliar at the same time. His head pounded as he slowly came to consciousness. As he became more aware of his thoughts, he suddenly wondered. What was he doing lying down? Wasn't he in the middle of battle previously?

Kotaro shot up in bed, faster than he could ever remember, heart racing.

"Looks like you're finally awake."

He should have been startled by the sudden gravelly voice, but he wasn't. Slowly turning his head to the familiar voice, Kotaro eyed his surroundings. So this was where he was. In his brother's makeshift room, and on his bed. On the edge of Camp Broken Glass, deep in Garlean territory.

"How ya feelin?" the voice continued.

But before he could respond, suddenly, all the memories came rushing back in. The feeling of waking up in an alien body. His limbs were not moving quite the way he wanted them to. The stupid weight of the sword in his right arm. Of reaching into his boot out of instinct and not finding the trusty bootknife there. The inability to feel half his body after an untimely explosion in enemy territory. Dragging a half dead body across the fucking cold snow. The fear of not being able to make it back to base camp in time.

Alisaie. G'raha. He'd just barely made it.

The relief and nausea he felt at his soul finally returning back to his own body. But mostly, relief. They hadn't died yet, _not like Haurchefant._

He let out a harsh breath, and made a snap decision then and there. No more running. No more excuses. No matter that it was a sudden and probably questionable one, made in the wake of a barely averted disaster.

"Esti. I've made up my mind," he responded instead, turning to face his brother, whose brows were now arched in confusion. "I'm gonna properly learn how to brawl again."

"Ha? And where in the seven hells did that come from? You didn't bloody answer my original question." There was now a huge scowl on Estinien's face, much more than his usual.

There was a short pause.

Then, Kotaro shuddered. He didn't want to directly answer this question, either. Instead, he said something else.

"I ... I don't want any of my comrades to die in front of my eyes ever again."

He didn't want to say more, for now.

The memories were still too raw, despite it already being about _two fucking years_. He silently hoped that Estinien would understand. After all, Estinien had also been there when Haurchefant was struck. And he had known they were thick as thieves. But even so, he hadn't been there to see firsthand the fiasco that happened the day before. Nor the moment when things went very wrong on The First, everything spiralling out of control.

Estinien gave him a very hard look, and he knew then and there that he wasn't going to be let off easy.

"Why hand to hand combat after all these years with magicks and light weapons? You were so against it previously. And you didn't seem like you were having any issues using a short sword or a lance, even if you couldn't swing a broadsword or a war axe."

_Too astute. He makes a very good point._ It was mostly fine, until it wasn't.

"...Maybe it's easier if I show you, that is, if you're willing? It's not ... gonna be pleasant though, I think. Come here." Kotaro responded carefully, a huge grimace on his face, and stretched out his arm, the soft glow of aether already swirling around his fingers.

Estinien looked puzzled for a moment, then sighed, recognizing it for what it was. Finally, he came over to sit on the bed. Kotaro placed his hand on his forehead, and started channeling more of whatever had been brewing in his palm.

He felt it then. All the flashbacks. It had only been for a short moment, but it had felt like forever.

Kotaro's time on The First. The absolutely sickening and revolting feeling all over, trying to claw out of his skin. The feeling of everything splitting apart. Light aether spilling everywhere, everything way too bright, vomiting out light as well. The inability to grip onto anything, physical nor magical, all faculties lost.

_Halone's tits, was aether sickness supposed to feel like this? It's clearly much worse than the experimental aetheryte they had to brave at Yedlihmad._

Then, the memory shifted.

Stuck in a hunk of heavy metal. Inability to use magic, simply because this body belonged to a Garlean. The weight of the steel gripped in his hands. The explosions, the inability to move half of himself as the armor dragged itself across the cold wasteland filled with nothing but a blizzard, smoke billowing from ceruleum burning all around.

_The memory of yesterday._

And then the visions abruptly ended.

Estinien gasped, tilting backwards in barely concealed shock. He saw it now.

"I see, for moments where magic and weapons are impossible."

But still, to think that there were worse moments than the massacre at Rhalgr's Reach, which he'd heard from passing rumors at the taverns.

"Aye," Kotaro sighed bitterly. "So much for all those forbidden magicks I learnt though." The ability to summon Leviathan and Ravana as egis? Virtually unheard of.

Upon hearing that, Estinien shot him a sharp look.

"You've been reckless since Haurchefant's death, and you know it," Estinien accused.

Kotaro winced. Estinien was right, he knew it, felt it, but couldn't stop. The memory of Haurchefant and his love for him may have filled his heart with warmth once, giving him strength to push ahead against all odds. For the longest time, his final words burned in his mind the brightest - _A smile better suits a hero_. He knew he was twisting the memory, but desperation had clung to him like a snake coiled deep around his skin. The need to protect what remained, letting nothing else slip past his fingers. The weight of the mantle he bore. _The burden of being the Warrior of Light. Of having to press on and be alright no matter the circumstances._

Not to mention, the world was ending - so many unknown monsters appearing from thin air, thralls made of people in its wake. _And it wasn't going to save itself._

"Yer not seriously askin' me to ferget him, are ya? Because... Even if th' stars fall, even if the world forgets him... I won't," Kotaro said, his voice low, eyes cast downward. _"I can't."_ He knows that his Lominsan accent is slipping in, but he couldn't bring himself to care.

Then, to his own surprise, buried memories rose unbidden, ones he hadn't thought about in a long time - Haurchefant's easy grin, the way he flirted without effort, his unwavering kindness. Those clear blue eyes. Always looking straight at his intently, eager to hear every single word of his adventures tumbling out of his mouth.

"And, despite 'verythin, I'm still here," Kotaro continued, forcing his voice to steady. "While I breathe, I have t'keep moving. It's my duty t'save the world. _No matter what._"

Kotaro's words rang heavy, the final three words punctuating the air as silence settled between them.

Estinien's gaze lingered on him a moment longer, thoughtful. He closed his eyes. Then he spoke, quieter than before.

"For the longest time, I thought it was despair that drove you. That you couldn't let go of his death."

Opening his eyes once again, he paused, his voice softening with a rare kind of honesty.

"But if that were true... you and Alphinaud wouldn't have gone through the madness of saving me, all those years ago."

Kotaro looked up, a little surprised by the shift in the conversation, and not quite sure where this was going.

Estinien stepped forward, placing his hand on his shoulder, then gripping firmly.

"Kotaro. I won't pretend I fully understand your duty... or the weight you carry. But you don't have to carry it alone."

A small, almost wry smile touched his lips.

"We made an oath. We're more than just _comrades_. And I've got your back, in whatever form it needs to be... brother."

Kotaro stared wide-eyed at him. The thought had never truly occurred to him before.

He'd always known Estinien had his back in battle - that much was certain. But the idea that it extended beyond the field? That Estinien meant it, not just in the thrill of the fight but in everything else?

That had been harder to grasp.

Estinien rarely said much. He let his actions speak for him - but even those were hard to decipher at times. He came and went like the wind, following his own instincts, vanishing for months without a word. It almost seemed like he shunned any form of responsibility that wasn't on his own terms. Tataru and Krile had to chase him all the way to Kugane just to get him to do one thing for them. And Kotaro, knowing this, never bothered about asking for more than he was willing to give.

And yet...

When he'd least expected, Estinien had been there.

He'd been there at Ghimlyt Dark, cutting a path through chaos to reach him when he was out cold at the most inopportune moment in his battle with Zenos. He'd been there behind the scenes, chasing down the remnants of Nidhogg's eye, finally vanquishing it in the wake of Shinryu's fall.

He hadn't forgotten.

No matter how unpredictable Estinien was, he always came through in the most unexpected of ways.

Kotaro couldn't deny the quiet trust they'd forged since back in Dravania. But with everything in the world falling apart, he'd been swept from one crisis or war to the next, with little time to think, let alone feel, what it was that was that they truly had with each other, outside of the oath they'd sworn to protect each other in battle.

Until now.

Seeing that he took too long to respond, Estinien smirked and continued cheekily, "Coeurl got your tongue?"

Kotaro let out a breath he didn't know he was holding. Who knew it'd be so cathartic to come to the realisation that someone's got your back in all manner of ways?

It was... definitely going to take time to let it all sink in, but his brother was right. He was here, and they were in it together for the long haul. The world might be ending, but they would challenge fate. Together.

"Say, why don't we share a toast? I wouldn't mind a reminder of our oath," Kotaro smirked in return.

"You just want an excuse to drink, don't you?" grumbled Estinien, but he acquiesced all the same.
