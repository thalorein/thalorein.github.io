The days pass by in a quick blur for F'ynn, as he splits his time evenly on honing his skills with the broadsword in Thanalan and La Noscea, and digging for clues around the outskirts of Coerthas. By then, F'ynn had come to know a few key Ishgardians, mostly Lord Haurchefant and Drillemont, helping them with small skirmishes with dragons, sussing out a herectic right in the middle of Whitebrim Front. He even managed to help him with re-taking Stone Vigil, an outpost near Whitebrim Front, long occupied by dragons, having earned his trust.

But then came the turning point with Fray. 

It was just another one of those routine afternoons - this time spent helping Lord Drillemont with a giant problem in Daniffen Pass. Business as usual, deal with the problem and head back. But just was he was done with dealing with the giants, a runner approached, looking like he'd run from a wild coeurl, completely out of breath. His eyes were glazed over too.

"Sir! Apologies for the sudden intrusion. Lord Drillemont urgently requests you meet him with all haste!" the runner heaves out. 

"What's the meaning of this?!" the knight beside F'ynn cuts in angrily.

"Certain...allegations have been made about our hero - they were seen conversing with a corpse in the Brume, by Temple Knights no less...Not that anyone would believe such slander, but..."

*So, the temple knights strike again.* This is worrying. But more importantly, found conversing with a corpse, huh? In recent days, he'd been puzzled by the reactions of some people he'd been helping around and about. Looking at him like he'd been out of his mind, when Fray had said something condescending to them, or when he'd turned around momentarily to speak with Fray himself. It was as if Fray didn't exist. Did that mean that he had been speaking with someone imaginary? Then who said the words which made people look at him with wide, fearful eyes? It was a conundrum.

F'ynn nods, picks up his pace, and starts sprinting back to Whitebrim Front. As he neared, he spotted Drillemont heading up towards him.

"They might want you to surrender your weapon," Drillemont announces grimly. 

There was no way in hell he was going to do that, at least not to corruption incarnate. And not to mention, F'ynn couldn't trust anybody in Ishgard, at least as of yet. Drillemont seems to sense this as well.

"But I'll not allow that to happen. But quickly, we need to explain ourselves, they're waiting in the outpost itself."

As they ran into the encampment itself, what they saw shocked them to the very core. A massacre of temple knights, all felled in broad daylight, in the middle of the camp. And standing right in the center of the pile of bodies was Fray.

He turns around to face F'ynn, unhidden fury in his eyes. 

"Stop denying the truth in front of you!" Fray says, his eyes slowly turning flaming red. "Call my name! my true name! you know what it is! You knew that the real Fray was dead from the beginning!"

F'ynn stares at him, uncomprehending. So Fray continues his speech.

"You knew that you just wanted a mentor. Someone to teach you new techniques. Someone who is free to do and say things you would not. Look at me! Don't you see who I am?"

F'ynn's honestly not sure yet, but he decides then and there that he has an important question to ask Drillemont.

"Lord Drillemont. Forgive this rudeness - but there's something that's been on my mind, and I must know the truth - is there anyone standing in front of me, right here, right now?" F'ynn's voice was even, but it brooked no argument.

Drillemont stares ahead, first slightly to his left, then his right, and frowns deeply. Then, very reluctantly and carefully, he answers, "No, F'ynn... I do not see anyone."

So, this confirms it. He had been hallucinating. A low and bitter chuckle left his throat. He is going mad, isn't he?
Unable to hold it in anymore, his laughter steadily burst forth, loud and mocking, echoing through the entire encampment.

Before he can process anything else around him however, swirls of dark shadows unexpectedly erupts all around him. He feels the aether in the air around him coalescing into a midpoint until - BOOM! A dark and huge cloud bursts forth, and scatters with the wind. And in the midst of it all, he sees it - a shadow of himself, with glowing red eyes, standing right there, in the center of where the cloud had been. *Where Fray had been. So this is what he meant.*

"Everything up till now has been your story, but I will win, and then it will be mine!" his dark self declares, instantly launching into an offensive strike.

F'ynn just barely parries it - of course they'd be an equal in match, *it was himself after all*. 

Throwing a shadow wall around himself, he spends the first few moments eyeing the movements of his opponent, mostly dodging, and observing. This version of himself was on full offsense, which meant he needed to be quick too, to land any hits.

*Time to make a move.* 

He throws some unmends and soul eaters, while dodging around the blade of his other self. It seemed like this would never end - they were too evenly matched, and he was staring to tire.

Drillemont seemed to have noticed this, because he suddenly yells around the din of all the fighting and heavy clanking of metal swords ringing through the air, "Pull yourself together, lad! I know you're stronger than this!"

*Ugh,* F'ynn thinks. But he can't let the old geezer down.

With that, F'ynn sharpens his focus, trying to overpower his other self again. But then, his dark self changes tactics, and summons dark sprites around the camp. There were too many, and no way he could focus on all of them at the same time.

"Focus on your battle, I'll handle them!" Drillemont yells again, charging towards a sprite.

It feels strange, having someone who had his back in battle. He'd always done everything on his own, and he's not even sure if he trusts Drillemont despite the old man's trust in him. But he didn't have a choice here, did he? It was that, or being overwhelmed by numbers. So, he heeded his words.

"Tch, friends? Really?" his dark self scoffs. He says it with so much scorn, but Fynn sees it in his eyes - anger and resentment. *No one's got their back.* It's a shocking realization that he knows this feeling, but there's no time to dwell on it. They continue to clash against each other, a fury of shadow swipes and slashes.

Then, the battle comes to a head. His dark self jumps a fair distance back, and begins casting an enormous spell. With dawning horror, F'ynn realises what this spell is - a gravity force, long casting, with a huge area of effect. 

Shit. There was nowhere to dodge.

He panicks and loses his composure for a split second, until a thought hit him. He shouldn't know what this skill was, he hadn't learnt it, *but his mind knew.* The side effects though? He didn't know. But there was no more time to hesitate, it was now or never. He casts the spell, feeling the pull of the aether from his body, twisting in a strange way. He feels it then - pain lancing up his arms, and in his skull. He grits his teeth, and just right at the moment, the gravity force slams into him, pushing his weight onto the ground. Fuck, the force of the attack was horrible even if he'd just narrowly survived it, but the skill he'd just used -  Living Dead - was a far bigger hell that he was currently contending with. 

Whoever the fuck created this skill wasn't getting his eternal thanks, but damnation in everlasting purgatory.

But at the moment, he has more important things to worry about. He knows he has to end things quickly, the sooner the better - Living Dead wasn't only eating at his aether, it was also eating at his sanity, and would slowly but surely turn him into a walking corpse if he spent too long trapped in this battle against himself. But where would he find the opening?

Time seems to pass by in agonizing slowness, but eventually, the crushing effect of the gravity lifts. Enough for him to lift his head, and he sees it right then and there, his opening - his dark self is on his knees, all but bent over in exhaustion. It was time to strike. 

He leaps, and lands the finishing blow with an uppercut strike, sending his other self flying across the snow and flat onto his stomach. His other self struggles to push himself up on his arms, but ultimately gives up, his head tilting up towards him weakly, in defiance. 

"...Looks like it ends here for me. No matter - they've now borne witness to the darkness within you," his dark half grits out, slightly grinning and revelling in his own victory.

F'ynn furrows his brows, and gives him a cold glare. But no matter what he intends to do, his other self was right - there was no escaping what just happened today, and likely, he'd end up being detained by temple knights, having to fight in a trial of fire.

But fate seemed to have something else in store for him.
To his surprise, the knights of Whitebrim and Drillemont slowly surrounds him, kneeling, then placing their hands on his shoulders. Then, they back him up. 

"After all the help you've rendered to us, we'll always support you!"

This... was not what he expected. And from the corner of his eyes, he could see that this was not what his dark self envisioned happening either. While a faint glimmer of hope sparks in his heart, he sees the fight leaves the eyes of his other self, hopes crushed. Somehow, F'ynn finds the empathetic part of himself feeling his heart wrenching at the sight.

"I guess... I will never be free, huh?", his dark self mumbles, head drooping to the ground, eyes filled with sorrow.

F'ynn gasps, a bolt of realization striking him then. Why didn't he think of this before?

This *is* him. His righteous anger, bitterness and hatred towards many things in life. All condensed and suppressed into the deepest parts of his mind. Never to see the light of day. His darkness, hidden, to be forgotten in the dust, until it was brought forth by the aether in the Dark Knight soul stone. *Begging to be acknowledged.*

He takes a deep breath, and slowly, he steps forward, right in front of his dark half.

"I understand now", F'ynn says as he holds out his right arm, palm open in a gesture of peace.

"I am you. And you are me. We are two opposing halves of the same person."

His dark half freezes upon hearing the words. Should he dare to hope? Cautiously and reluctantly, he looks up to see for himself. Expecting to see fear and maybe even rejection. He was met with nothing except unwavering conviction written in the red glassy eyes of F'ynn's determined gaze.
 
"But, we can both co-exist. And, from now on, I will listen to you. We will face the future together, *even if we fall far enough to receive the judgement of Nald'thal's flames*," he continues, his voice strong and steady.

*So, this is his answer.* That... wasn't what he expected. Even so... this is a good outcome, isn't it?

Unsteadily, his dark half pushes himself up to a kneeling position, left arm gripping his Zantetsuken, which had been lodged deep into the snow, to stabilize himself. Then, with his right arm, he finally claps the outstretched arm in a firm grip. 

"Aye, together," he replies with a small smile, finally disappearing into a shimmering swirl of dust.

For the first time in a long time, F'ynn feels the weight of the world leave his heart, leaving nothing but peace in its wake. He rounds his fingers into fists, carefully bringing it close to his chest, as if it was a fragile thing. *He will not run from himself ever again.* 

Then, he finally opens his eyes. The surroundings were clear once again, free of the cloud of dark aether. To his surprise, a pile of armor, the very same ones he saw on that very fateful first day with Fray, lay there. 

Drillemont walks up to him, a grim expression on this face.

"Can't say I expected this turn of events. I guess this must be the friend you were referring to earlier", he says, his voice serious and gravelly.

F'ynn nods solemnly.

"I'll make sure this friend of yours gets a proper burial", he says as response, before turning his back, heading back to the barracks.

"Thank you for your kindness, Lord Drillemont," Fynn replies softly, relieved, as he gives a deep bow.

Then, Drillemont abruptly stops and turns back to face him.

"Oh and F'ynn?" he calls.

F'ynn peers back up, his eyebrows arching, surprised to hear more words.

"No one here shall speak of the incident that happened right here at Whitebrim, today. I give you my promise on that."
