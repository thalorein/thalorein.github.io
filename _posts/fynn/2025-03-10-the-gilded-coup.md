> Setting: After "Before the Fall: The Parting Glass" quest, outside the Royal Palace
{: .note }

F'ynn sensed something was amiss even before anything had happened. Call it a warrior's instincts, not that he was any mighty warrior himself. He didn't trust many things, but he trusted his instincts - they'd serve him well time and time again.

He peered out of the window closest to his cot in the barracks. So many Brass Blades, moving through the city, into the Royal Palace. That was not... a good sign.

*What are the Monetarists up to this time?* F'ynn wondered to himself.

With that sort of unexpected event happening, he decided then and there that he should gear up, right now. Better to be prepared than sorry, he thought as he started to attach his pauldrons to his armor. He grabbed his sword and shield, which had been standing right by his armorie. Strapping the shield onto his back, he stared at his sword for a long moment. *Zantetsuken* - a blade as black as the cloud of darkness, a sword he'd pried from the hands of the primal Odin when he was out on a hunt in the Black Shroud. Why he had done so, he had no clue himself except for the foreboding that something about it had pulled him in. It was also a mysterious sword, one that could shrink or lengthen at will. Right now, it was the length of a long sword, perfect for swinging single-handedly. But the moment he strapped it onto his belt, it shrunk until all that was left was the hilt. Finally ready, he headed out of the barracks into the main hall of the Immortal Flames, where familiar long silver hair swung into his gaze almost immediately. The timing was eeriely impeccable.

Pipin had suddenly burst through the halls of the Immortal Flames, hurried and out of breath. He scanned the hall quickly, his eyes seemingly looking for someone in particular. Then it flitted towards him and locked on. Heaving a sigh of relief, he immediately strode to him.

"F'ynn. It's good to see you. However, unfortunate times call for unfortunate measures. We have to make haste - the Sultana has been assassinated, and my Father has been captured by the Monetarists for regicide."

Thal's balls. The Sultana, dead? What high form of treachery had just taken place in the Palace? His eyes forlon, he tilted his head upwards as he gazed to the shimmering but also foreboding jewel of Ul'dah, also known as the Royal Palace. A truly tragic sequence of events, for one who was made a puppet of a ruler at the mere age of five, whose parents also tragically died then. The coincidence of the timing only pointed to one thing - her parents had likely been assassinated, even if all the available evidence suggested otherwise.

And General Raubahn, captured for regicide? He knew the man to be principled, although somewhat intense. Something didn't quite sit right.

But his thoughts also started to stray - what about the Sultansworn? They were the elite guards of the Sultana. Wasn't it their job to made sure their charge came to no harm? He felt an extremely bitter taste on his tongue, made worse by the atrocity that Jenlyns had but recently committed against him.

Pipin continued, seemingly oblivious to his inner conflict, his voice lowered to a whisper. "I believe we've been framed. Sympathizers of the Royalists will be harshly dealt with at the moment, and we should lay low for now."

F'ynn understood the implications. 

The bulk of the Immortal Flames was made up of mercenaries. But besides that, a tiny portion was also made up of the Sultansworn. *And they were all supposed to be loyal to Raubahn and Nanamo*. Granted, they were not as organised or unified as the other Grand Companies in Eorzea, which led to the reason why most of its members still had not been disturbed by any from the opposing faction. 

Not one to dwell overlong on the past, his eyes widened a fraction when a sudden memory came to him, and he remembered how he had joined the Immortal Flames - through a recruitment notice. But his decision had been cemented way before then, from the moment he'd seen how the gears of fate had brought him to see what he saw in Pipin, Raubahn and Nanamo. This reinforced his resolve - he had no love for the Monetarists nor their armed forces, the Brass Blades. *Never had, and never will be.*

"I will investigate what has been happening and try to locate Father. In the meantime, I'd like you to meet up with Papashan. There's the truth of another matter that we would like you to look into that requires utmost discretion."

F'ynn raised his right arm across his chest, his fist tightend in a ball. "May Nald'thal bless you with fortune," F'ynn responded softly, giving the traditional Flame Salute. Without another word, he turned and strode from the hall, resolved to uncover the truth - just as he was wont to do.
