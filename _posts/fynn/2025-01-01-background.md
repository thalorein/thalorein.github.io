## Notes
I originally created F'ynn back in 2021 as a means to re-play the MSQ and try to understand all the story parts I didn't understand before. But as I went through ARR, I started thinking of how F'ynn would react to various things in the story based on his starting point (Ul'dah) and the brainworms just grew into what it is now.

F'ynn's story generally feels a lot more cohesive to me compared to Kota because I find it much more straightforward to write about the way he thinks, while Kota is just this unpredictable ball of fur rolling wherever he pleases - but one day, I will get to the bottom of Kota.
